FatherCare — Blood Sugar Summary

Health advice

Important: follow the father's clinician-approved plan for medicines, meals, glucose targets, and exercise. Do not stop, change, or double medication based on this dashboard.

Daily habits

  • Take prescribed medicine at the prescribed dose and time. Follow instructions about food.
  • Eat regular, balanced meals. Include vegetables, pulses or other suitable protein, and high-fibre foods. Follow any meal plan provided by the clinician.
  • Drink water regularly unless a clinician has advised a fluid restriction.
  • Choose comfortable walking or exercise and build up gradually. Carry a phone and any clinician-recommended fast-acting sugar source if at risk of low blood sugar.
  • Check feet for rubbing, cuts, or blisters, especially after walking or jogging. Use suitable footwear.
  • Keep a record of readings, meal context, symptoms, and medicine questions to discuss at appointments.
  • Prioritise sleep and recovery. If unwell, dizzy, unusually weak, or experiencing symptoms of low or high blood sugar, pause exercise and follow the clinician's sick-day plan.

When to seek help

Get urgent medical help for severe confusion, fainting, trouble breathing, chest pain, or other serious symptoms. Contact the clinician promptly for repeated readings outside the personal target range or repeated low readings.

Advice here is general education and is not a substitute for personal medical care.
